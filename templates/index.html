Why Explainable AI in Cybersecurity? Traditional ML models provide predictions without explaining the underlying attack indicators. SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) utilizes cooperative game theory to quantify the exact mathematical contribution of each lexical, structural, and content feature, empowering SOC teams to validate automated actions.
